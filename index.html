Snowdoku!

Find the sleeping huskies. Snowdoku! is a cozy logic puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Sleeping huskies are hidden under the snow: one in every row, every column and every colored snow patch, and they never touch, not even corner to corner. Every snowfield can be solved with pure logic. No guessing, ever.

🐾 Endless levels that grow from 5×5 to 10×10 and Double Pack puzzles
❄️ A new Daily Snowfield every day
🌌 Aurora skies, gentle sounds and 3D huskies that react to every move
