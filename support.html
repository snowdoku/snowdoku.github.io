Support

Need a hand? Email snowdoku.support@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days.

How do I play?

Each row, each column and each colored snow patch hides one sleeping husky (two on Double Pack levels), and huskies never touch, not even diagonally. Tap or drag to mark ✕ where no husky can be, and double-tap a tile to dig one up. Stuck? Tap the nose for a hint that explains the next step. You'll find the full rules in Settings › How to play.

What are sniffs?

Sniffs are hints. The first few levels have unlimited sniffs. After that, every snowfield comes with 2 free sniffs, and you can watch a short video for another one, or get unlimited sniffs with Snowdoku+.

What does Snowdoku+ include?

No ads, unlimited sniffs, every past Daily Snowfield, and free snowflake restores. It's available monthly or yearly (the yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers). There's also a one-time Remove Ads purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Snowdoku+ and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

I bought something but it isn't showing up.

Open Snowdoku!, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases, making sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress?

Your progress is saved on your device and included in your iPhone or iPad backups. Deleting the app deletes its progress.
