Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the Snowdoku! app ("Snowdoku!", "we", "us") handles and why. Snowdoku! is published by DYNSELL LLC. The short version: we don't have accounts, and we don't collect your personal information ourselves. Ads in the free version are provided by Google, which collects some data as described below.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, best times and streaks are stored only on your device (and in your device backups). We never receive this information.

Purchases

Remove Ads and Snowdoku+ are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks with Apple which purchases are active on your Apple Account so it can unlock them. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

If you haven't bought Remove Ads or Snowdoku+, Snowdoku! shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's software may collect:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks, and otherwise identifiers that aren't used across other companies' apps;
  • Your IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • Ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether Snowdoku! may track you. You can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you decline, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized using the advertising identifier.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: you'll see a consent form powered by Google, and you can change your choices anytime in Snowdoku! › Settings › Privacy Choices.
  • No ads: Remove Ads or Snowdoku+ turns off ads, and with them the ad-related data collection.

Children

Snowdoku! is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Because we don't hold your data, deleting the app removes everything Snowdoku! stored on your device. For data held by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls at myadcenter.google.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
