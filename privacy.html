Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information the Snowdoku! app ("Snowdoku!", "we", "us") handles and why. Snowdoku! is published by DYNSELL LLC. The short version: we don't have accounts, and we don't collect your personal information ourselves. Ads in the free version are provided by Google, which collects some data as described below.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, best times and streaks are stored only on your device (and in your device backups). We never receive this information, apart from the anonymous statistics described next.

Anonymous usage statistics (TelemetryDeck)

To learn which puzzles are too hard, where players get stuck and which features they enjoy, Snowdoku! sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the EU. This is everything they contain:

  • How you use the game (one event for each of these moments, with only the details named):
    • a level started (level number, board size, whether it's a Double Pack board, which place on the trail), solved (level, seconds taken, mistakes, hints, whether it was perfect) or left unfinished (level, seconds, how far along in percent, and whether you went Home, left the app or restarted);
    • a hint or sniff was used (level, and whether it was free, paid or from the pack), and you ran out of sniffs (level, and which option was offered: a video, the pack or Snowdoku+);
    • a Daily or Blizzard started or was solved (Daily or Blizzard, seconds, streak length, and whether it is your very first Daily), a streak was repaired, and a walk goal was reached (which goal);
    • the Den was opened, a Den item was bought (which item and its price in snowflakes, the in-game currency), and a pack member was rescued (which one);
    • the Snowdoku+ screen was shown (from where), and purchases were restored;
    • ads: an ad break was shown (where in the game), and a reward video was offered, accepted, watched to the end or couldn't play (where in the game, and which of those four);
    • a step of the first-time tutorial was reached (which step), a setting was switched on or off (which setting and its new value), the app was opened (fresh start or return from the background), and the Share button of a Daily result was tapped (from the win card or the results card).
  • Performance: how many milliseconds the app took to open.
  • Purchases: which in-app product (Remove Ads or a Snowdoku+ plan) you started to buy, bought, or couldn't buy. Never a price paid, payment details or your Apple Account.
  • Apple Ads install attribution: if the app was installed from an Apple Search Ads ad, the campaign, ad group and keyword numeric IDs are sent once, anonymously. To learn this, the app asks Apple once, on first launch, using an anonymous token from Apple's AdServices framework; it needs no tracking permission and uses no advertising identifier (IDFA). Nothing else from Apple's answer is kept.
  • When: the date and time each event was sent.
  • Basic app and device information that TelemetryDeck's software adds to every event: app and iOS version, device model, screen size, time zone, region, language and locale, light or dark mode, accessibility settings (such as larger text or reduced motion), and simple session counts (for example how many days the app was used).

All of it is handled the same way:

  • They are anonymous and not linked to you: TelemetryDeck receives only a salted, one-way hashed identifier, never your name, Apple Account, email, device name or advertising identifier (IDFA), and doesn't store your IP address.
  • They are not used for tracking, not combined with data from other apps or websites, and not used for ads.
  • They never contain anything you type.

See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

Purchases

Remove Ads and Snowdoku+ are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks with Apple which purchases are active on your Apple Account so it can unlock them. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

Unless you have Snowdoku+, Snowdoku! uses Google AdMob for ads: ad breaks between levels (until you buy Remove Ads) and optional reward videos you choose to watch (these stay available after Remove Ads). Snowdoku! never asks to track you, and its ads are not personalized. To show and measure ads, Google's software may collect:

  • Device identifiers. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is only available if you allowed tracking in an earlier version of Snowdoku!, which asked; this version no longer asks;
  • Your IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • Ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: you'll see a consent form powered by Google, and you can change your choices anytime in Snowdoku! › Settings › Privacy Choices.
  • Tracking from an earlier version: if you allowed tracking in an earlier version of Snowdoku!, you can turn it off anytime in your device's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Fewer ads: Remove Ads turns off ad breaks between levels; optional reward videos, and Google's software that provides them, stay available. Snowdoku+ turns off all ads, and Snowdoku! no longer starts Google's ad software.

Children

Snowdoku! is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Because we don't hold your personal data, deleting the app removes everything Snowdoku! stored on your device. The anonymous usage statistics can't be linked back to you, so there's nothing in them to look up or delete. For data held by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls at myadcenter.google.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
